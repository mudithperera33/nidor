CRITICAL HOMEPAGE FIX — NO OVERLAPPING SCENES

The new homepage looks good visually, BUT the scenes are overlapping during scroll.

I need the homepage to behave like a CINEMATIC SEQUENCE:

SCENE 1
↓
SCENE 1 completely exits
↓
SHORT TRANSITION
↓
SCENE 2
↓
SCENE 2 completely exits
↓
SHORT TRANSITION
↓
SCENE 3
...

NEVER show two primary scene compositions at the same time.

==================================================
CORE RULE
==================================================

ONLY ONE PRIMARY HOMEPAGE SCENE MAY BE VISIBLE AT A TIME.

When the next scene begins entering:

The previous scene MUST already be completely hidden
OR be in the final few moments of its exit.

Do NOT allow:

Scene 1 text + Scene 2 text
Scene 2 text + Scene 3 text
Scene 3 image + Scene 4 text
multiple fragrance information blocks
multiple collection descriptions

to remain readable simultaneously.

The homepage must feel like a controlled sequence of chapters.

==================================================
USE ENTER → HOLD → EXIT → TRANSITION
==================================================

Every homepage scene must follow this exact choreography:

1. ENTER
2. FULL COMPOSITION HOLD
3. EXIT
4. SHORT TRANSITION
5. NEXT SCENE ENTERS

Timeline concept:

0% ─────────────── 15%
        ENTER

15% ───────────────────────────── 80%
                 HOLD

80% ─────────────────── 95%
                 EXIT

95% ───────── 100%
       SHORT TRANSITION


Only after the previous scene has exited should the next scene become fully active.

==================================================
TEXT GROUPS MUST MOVE TOGETHER
==================================================

For every scene, treat all of its text as ONE composition.

For example:

SCENTS
STORIES
YOU
supporting paragraph

must be treated as one scene group.

Do NOT allow:

SCENTS from Scene A
+
STORIES from Scene A
+
YOU from Scene A
+
new text from Scene B

to coexist during the transition.

Group each scene's elements into a single wrapper/container.

Animate the GROUP rather than independently allowing each element to remain behind.

==================================================
IMPORTANT: NO GHOST TEXT
==================================================

When a scene exits:

- heading fades/moves out
- description fades/moves out
- labels disappear
- notes disappear
- decorative text associated with that scene disappears

Everything belonging to the scene must exit together.

Do not leave previous text floating behind the next scene.

==================================================
LANDSCAPE IMAGE SCENE
==================================================

The landscape image can transition into the next scene visually, BUT:

Do not allow the previous scene's text to remain visible over the next scene's text.

The image can act as a visual bridge.

TEXT CANNOT.

      +
SCENE 3 TEXT

then:

SCENE 3 TEXT exits completely

then:

SCENE 4 TEXT enters.
