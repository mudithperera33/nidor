SCROLL-SCRUBBED ANIMATION
==================================================

Keep the entire system scroll-controlled.

Do NOT use:

==================================================
IMPORTANT: DO NOT MAKE TRANSITIONS TOO FAST
==================================================

The current problem is not only overlap.

Some transitions feel visually rushed.

Give the eye time to process each scene.

For each major scene:

ENTER:
slow and smooth

HOLD:
long enough to read

EXIT:
smooth and deliberate

BREATHING:
short but noticeable

Do not make every scene use identical timing.

Text-heavy scenes need longer holds.

Simple visual scenes can move faster.
